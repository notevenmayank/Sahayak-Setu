Process

How it works

Three steps, and you can see the price at every one of them.

  1. 1

    Choose a service

    Browse verified workers and pick the one that fits — rating, distance and starting price are all on the card.

  2. 2

    Book a slot

    Set a date and time. The quote you confirm is the quote the worker sees.

  3. 3

    Job done, worker paid

    The worker completes the task and payment is released. The cooperative takes no cut for introducing you.

Why a cooperative

The people doing the work own the platform.

Every rupee above the worker's rate stays in the cooperative and funds verification, training and cover between jobs — instead of a commission.
